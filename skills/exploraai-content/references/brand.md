# Brand and business goals
Brand: ExploraAI | https://www.exploraai.com.br
Audience: Brazilian Portuguese-speaking travelers looking for inspiring destinations, guides, and lodging options in Brazil.
Brand voice: curious, practical, inviting, factual, simple Brazilian Portuguese. Avoid generic hype and exaggerated claims.
Primary funnel: social discovery -> account/profile -> destination guide or landing page -> accommodation search -> partner click.
Content mix starter hypothesis: 40% discovery, 25% comparisons, 20% travel tips, 15% planning/lodging. Rebalance after measured results.
Platform-specific tracking must identify source, medium, campaign and video ID. Verify actual landing pages exist before linking.
